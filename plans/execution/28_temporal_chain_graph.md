## Goal
A graph in the lake, keyed by period, that the app can browse.
- **Companies are leaves.** Each company page shows how its own business changed over time: revenue mix, applications, customers and suppliers, and events.
- **Industries are nodes.** A node is a taxonomy segment or product that holds its companies, weighted by revenue for each period.
- **Industries link to each other** through supplier / buyer edges and finance / invest edges. The edges are aggregated from company evidence for each period.

## Risks
- **Mentions vs real relations.** An IR answer saying "正在送样" is not a customer. Keep the stage (sample / qualified / volume) and default the edge to `named` without a share.
- **Text drift between years.** A name absent from next year's text is not a lost customer. Only top-5 shares and contracts count as hard evidence of change; the app labels prose changes as "提及变化".
- **Defining industry nodes.** Taxonomy products are too fine for a readable industry page; SW L3 is too coarse for the chain. See the open questions.
- **Volume.** Q&A is 129k items. Filter by keyword before the LLM. Ark is flat fee (D-122), but throughput limits wall time.
- **Prior leaking into evidence (D-097).** `node_edge` keeps `prior` and `evidenced` apart, and the app shows them differently.

## Open questions
- **Node level.** Should industry nodes be taxonomy *segments*, with products as sub-nodes the user can open? The alternative is SW L3 with the taxonomy inside it. Recommended: segments.
- **What "finance / invest" covers.**
  - Recommended: equity stakes (listed company holds a listed company), outside investments and M&A into another industry, and subsidiaries.
  - Possible extensions: supply-chain finance and receivables / prepayments; bank and fund ownership of an industry.
