## Goal
The model is named **散户温度计 RetailGauge** (user, 2026-11-13). A high reading means retail is entering; a low reading means retail is leaving. Model version: `retail_gauge_v5`.

Every trading day, each listed stock has a point-in-time estimate of how its shareholder count will change this quarter (retail entering or leaving), plus a signal row: large-order flow streaks, chip distribution, and the marginal effect on the user's factor exposures.

Every trading day, a short ranked opportunity list combines those signals with earnings inflection and valuation. It is shown in the app and sent in the morning report. The user asked for a daily list (2026-11-13), not a weekly one.

## Scope
- In:
  - Daily whole-market `cyq_perf` snapshot as a dataset.
  - Productionise the v5 shareholder nowcast from `research_space/prototypes/holder_nowcast/` (LightGBM; point-in-time expanding walk-forward; quarterly retrain; calibration to expected holder change with IQR).
  - Signal row per stock:
    - large-order (20–100万) weekly net flow and streak;
    - small-order streak;
    - margin trend;
    - chip winner rate and cost bands;
    - national-team ETF flow allocated by index weight, as context only.
  - Weekly opportunity list with rules in `config/discovery_rules.yaml` (shared with plan 16 ph3).
  - Columns on the holdings and watchlist pages and a section in the 7e morning report.
  - Evaluation once real holder counts are published.
  - Avoid flag (D-155): gauge pct ≥ `retail_gauge.avoid_pct` (0.9) marks 散户过热. It excludes the stock from the list and is shown on holdings, watchlist and the morning report.
  - Daily reading rules (D-167):
    - the raw daily reading is used, with no smoothing;
    - a streak counts readings on the same side at checkpoints t, t−3, t−6 … sessions;
    - `retail_leaving` = 3 checkpoints in a row ≤ 0.3;
    - `avoid_strong` = 3 checkpoints in a row ≥ 0.9; a single reading ≥ 0.9 stays the D-155 warning.
- Out:
  - Price-return forecasting or trading signals beyond the D-155 avoid flag and the phase 6 tests (the user: "不在乎预测股价"; phase 6 added 2026-11-18). No long/short or sizing rules.
  - Industry-neutral target or national-team features as model inputs; both gave no gain in the prototype.
  - Sequence models (CNN / LSTM), which are a later option.
  - New paid data.

## Risks
- The shareholder-count model drifts when the market regime changes (e.g. the January 2026 national-team exit). Mitigations: quarterly evaluation against published counts; an alert if a quarter's rank IC is below 0.4.
- Point-in-time leakage through restated holder counts or late announcements. Mitigations: use the first `ann_date` per (stock, quarter); the leakage test in ph3.
- `cyq_perf` is a higher Tushare tier and could be withdrawn. In the prototype chip features added only ~0.01 of IC, so the model degrades gracefully: missing chips are filled as NaN.
- The opportunity list is empty in a thin market (the user: "这个市场机会真少"). That is valid output; the list shows the near-misses with the missing condition rather than relaxing rules.
- The avoid flag's return evidence is 19 quarters, and the LightGBM params were tuned on 2023–2025. Mitigation: out-of-sample tracking in `RetailGaugeEvaluator`; review rule in D-155.
- Two new dependencies (lightgbm, scikit-learn) in the shared venv; peers need `uv sync` (D-141).
- Phase 6: the walk-forward gauge starts in 2022, the risk model and campaign in 2019; tests use the overlap only. A gauge in the risk model would also strip its alpha when the score is made risk-neutral; the report shows both effects before any decision.
